Evidence I Am Adding

My evidence will show how I understood the user's needs, developed ideas, created a solution and used feedback to improve it.

How This Shows My Skills

My Design Thinking evidence demonstrates my ability to understand a real problem, think creatively, develop solutions and improve my ideas through feedback.

Reflection

What problem was I trying to solve?

I wanted to understand a real problem and develop a practical solution based on people's needs.

How did I make sure the solution was human-centred?

I learned that feedback can reveal weaknesses in an idea and help me improve my solution.
